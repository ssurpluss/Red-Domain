# 🐐 Red Domain
### 🎮 **[Play Red Domain in Your Browser on itch.io](https://ssurpluss.itch.io/red-domain)**

**Dive into the world of Red Domain, a 2D Martian base-building and survival adventure game starring a SUPER-GOAT!**

Touchdown on the Red Planet in the **G.O.A.T.-1 Mars Lander**. No rescue is coming—design an air-tight habitat, manage your life-support systems, brave violent Martian weather, and rove across impact craters to survive **30 Martian minutes** on the frontier.

---

## 🚀 Gameplay Loop

1. **Cargo & Loadout Preparation**
   * Pack essential building materials, life-support machines, and survival gear while managing payload weight.
   * Expedition rations are strictly capped at **5 Water Modules** and **5 Grass Bales**, making surface exploration essential for long-term survival.

2. **Base Construction Phase**
   * Construct an air-tight shelter inside the **Blue Surveyor Grid** using Metal Walls and an Airlock Door.
   * Deploy **2×1 Solar Panels** outside in the **Amber Solar Zone** to generate power.
   * Mount critical appliances (**Air Purifier**, **Command Console**, **Food Tastifier**) directly against interior metal walls.

3. **30-Minute Martian Survival**
   * Monitor six real-time vitals: **HP**, **Oxygen (O₂)**, **Power**, **Food**, **Hydration (H₂O)**, and **Radiation**.
   * **Red Dust Storms** sweep across the surface, shattering outdoor solar panels and cutting off base power.
   * **Marsquakes** fracture the Martian crust and crack habitat walls, causing hull breaches and radiation leaks.
   * Drive the **Mars Rover** out to fallen meteorite crash sites to scavenge **Meteor Rations**, **Cryo Ice Flasks**, and **O₂ Canisters**.

---

## 🛠️ Features

* **Procedural 2D Pixel Art & Audio:** Custom pixel-art tiles, sprites, weather overlays, and synthesized retro sound effects.
* **Wire & Hull Repair Mini-Game:** Equip **Super Goat-Glue 4000** to weld broken circuits and seal hull breaches on damaged walls and solar panels.
* **Rover Exploration & Meteorite Drops:** Unannounced meteorites streak across the sky and crash into the Martian plains, rewarding bold expeditions with high-tier supplies.
* **Super-Goat Tech:** Equip the **Radiation Shield** for surface walks or power up the **Food Tastifier** to boost meal nutrition by +60%.

## 🎮 Controls

| Key / Input | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`W` `A` `S` `D` / Arrow Keys** | Move Goat / Drive Mars Rover |
| **`Left Click` (or Drag)** | Place selected structure in the build grid |
| **`Right Click` or `F`** | Toggle Delete Mode / Pick up placed items |
| **`1` – `9` / Scroll / `Z` `X`** | Switch hotbar items |
| **`E`** | Interact (Command Console, Board/Exit Rover, Salvage Meteorites, Eat/Drink) |
| **`R`** | Open Wire & Hull Repair Puzzle (while holding Super Goat-Glue 4000) |
| **`C`** | Consume selected food, water, or O₂ canister in Survival Mode |
| **`Tab`** | Toggle simulation speed (`1x` / `3x`) |
| **`Esc`** | Close active menus and popups |
